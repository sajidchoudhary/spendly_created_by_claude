Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register, along with the expense records you enter — amount, category, date, and description.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate the service: authenticating your account, saving and displaying your expenses, and generating summaries and breakdowns of your spending. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data is stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request that your account and associated data be deleted at any time, after which it will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party services except where required to operate the app (such as hosting infrastructure) or where required by law. Any such providers are bound to handle your data securely and only for the purposes we specify.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
